# Hidden Bridge Audit

The v0.15 ontology repeatedly relied on six notions that were neither fully supplied by its metalanguage nor represented by stable typed Relations. Because those notions connected otherwise dependency-ordered definitions, they could introduce additional commitments without appearing in the registry.

This audit applies the termhood challenge separately. Three notions are genuine object-language Relations. Two belong to the declared metalanguage but require explicit object witnesses whenever used. One duplicates an existing institutional Relation.

| Candidate bridge | Resolution | Binding consequence |
|---|---|---|
| identity criterion | declared metalanguage with mandatory Invariant and Persistence projection | Entity Claims must name ordered States, identity Invariant, and Persistence witness; sameness in the metalanguage supplies no Entity fact |
| denotation and use | Denotation promoted; generic use eliminated | Representation requires an ordered expression-target Denotation; semantic, causal, interpretive, instrumental, and institutional uses must name their distinct Relations |
| causal contribution | Causal Contribution promoted | causal efficacy requires two matched nonempty paths, a named input Difference, and a downstream Change; occurrence and precedence are insufficient |
| modal capability | Constraint-relative possibility declared as metalinguistic satisfiability | Capability becomes a constructive Specification whose positive classifications supply a satisfying Configuration and Action-producing path under named Constraints |
| support | Evidential Bearing promoted | supporting, defeating, and underdetermining are outputs of an evaluation Rule recorded by an Order for Evidence and Claim within a Scope |
| institutional eligibility | reduced to Standing | every eligibility Claim must resolve to Standing in a named Order, Rule, and Scope; no parallel eligibility predicate exists |

## Strongest rejected reductions

Denotation is not generic Relation alone because expression and target positions are load-bearing and asymmetric in Truth, Representation, Sign, and Symbol. It is not Interpretation because a Denotation can obtain without an Entity acting on it.

Causal Contribution is not Causal-path membership because an inert or redundant Presence may occur in a path without making a Difference to its endpoint. It is not Feeds alone because contribution Claims compare a named upstream Difference with a downstream Change across matched paths.

Evidential Bearing is not Evidence because Organon's Evidence is already an admitted Observation with scoped independence, while the same Evidence may support, defeat, or underdetermine a Claim under different evaluation Rules or Scopes. It is not Truth because a false Claim may receive supportive bearing.

Identity criterion does not become a fourth term. Identity is already supplied by the metalanguage, and Entity objectifies its cross-State application through an Invariant and Persistence witness. A free Identity Criterion object would merely rename that join unless Organon later needs to compare competing criteria as first-class Presences.

Relative possibility does not become Possibility. The only load-bearing modal use in the current registry is constrained satisfiability: a Capability classifies an Action only under a stated Constraint interpretation and constructive witness. Unrestricted metaphysical modality remains outside Organon.

Institutional eligibility does not become a term because Standing already states the complete Order-, Rule-, Entity-, status-, and Scope-indexed Relation. A second predicate would allow eligibility and Standing to drift without explaining the difference.

## Intellectual shadows

Identity criteria inherit their local burden from David Wiggins's work on sameness and persistence: reidentification is criterion-governed, not supplied by resemblance alone. Denotation inherits Frege's discipline of separating an expression from what it designates, without importing his full sense-reference theory. Constraint-relative possibility is locally shadowed by Kripkean model-relative satisfaction and Martin-Löf's proof-as-inhabitant discipline; Organon uses neither unrestricted possible worlds nor constructive logic as a whole.

Causal Contribution is locally shadowed by David Lewis's counterfactual dependence and James Woodward's interventionist comparison of changed inputs and outputs. Organon's paired paths are a finite typed test for one named Difference under declared matching conditions, not a complete theory of causation. Evidential Bearing is locally shadowed by Stephen Toulmin's separation of data, warrant, and claim; Organon instead makes the evaluation Rule and institutional Record explicit and does not inherit Toulmin's full argument schema.

Standing already belongs to the Searlean institutional shadow documented in the binding ontology. Reducing eligibility to Standing preserves that inheritance without multiplying status predicates. The contribution of this audit is not any local bridge by itself, but forcing all six to cross the same dependency and anti-collapse boundary as the rest of Organon.

## Collapse audit

- Denotation does not entail identity, fidelity, Truth, Interpretation, Operationalization, or Causal Contribution.
- Causal-path membership, temporal precedence, correlation, and downstream occurrence do not entail Causal Contribution.
- Capability does not entail completed Action, Permission, Authority, Standing, favorable Consequence, or prediction that the Action will occur.
- Evidence does not entail supportive Evidential Bearing; supportive Evidential Bearing does not entail Truth; Truth does not entail any Evidential Bearing.
- Standing does not entail Capability, Permission, Authority, Recognition by another Order, or actual institutional Action.
- A metalinguistic identity criterion does not entail that any Configuration satisfies it or persists as an Entity.

## Formal evidence and boundary

The Lean shadow contains an unequal Denotation witness, a Causal Contribution over two single-step paths whose inputs differ in one typed feature while sharing their typed context and whose outputs differ, a Capability whose possibility predicate is definitionally the inhabitance of its declared realization type, a typed Standing Relation, and supportive Evidential Bearing for a false Claim. Trust now carries the canonical paired-path contribution rather than a separate one-path predicate. Warranted Knowledge carries the exact supportive bearing rather than a free `supports` predicate.

The shadow does not prove that every natural-language expression has a unique Denotation, that the chosen comparison fixes every causally relevant variable, that a concrete Capability realization contains the binding Configuration and Action-producing Causal path, that every Capability procedure faithfully models its Environment, or that an Order's evaluation Rule is epistemically good. Those are evidence and parity gates, not hidden assumptions.

## Binding effect

C24 makes bridge elision a consistency violation. Future definitions may refine these Relations, but they may not use “same,” “means,” “causes,” “can,” “supports,” or “eligible” as untyped substitutes for the required witness structures.
