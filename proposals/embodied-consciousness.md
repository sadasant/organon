# Embodiment and Recurrent Consciousness Candidate

## Verdict

The embodiment cluster conforms after an adversarial repair that makes its load-bearing joins explicit. Body is a Relation between an Entity and state-indexed constituent Configurations; it does not receive an Entity-like identity of its own. Bodily Organization requires exact constituent membership and an inter-Organ causal feed. Embodied Perspective joins represented conditions and available Transformations to exact Denotations and upstream Differences. Recurrent Integration requires exact family membership for both comparison paths and both downstream Changes. Internal Activity Selection joins its representational Difference to the exact upstream Difference of the selecting Causal Contribution.

The consciousness sentence conforms only as a **candidate condition** within the existing consciousness protocol. It does not settle what Consciousness universally is, which Entities instantiate it, or what Evidence would decide such a Claim. Organon v0.19 therefore partially promotes the five supporting terms while the underlying consciousness condition remains quarantined.

This dossier does not address Attention, Love, Care, Respect, embodied self-governance, or Intention. Those terms require their own anti-collapse and formal review after the embodiment foundation is settled.

## Proposed definitions

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-D1 type=proposed_definition -->
**EC-D1 — Body:** A Body is a scoped Relation joining an Entity to a nonempty ordered sequence of constituent Configurations indexed to named States in that Entity's Persistence history. At every indexed State, the related Configuration contains at least one constituent. Recurring Transformations assigned to the Relation occur within the Entity's Interior under a Specification, are admitted by the Constraints of the Entity's Boundary, occur in named Causal paths, and preserve the Entity's identity Invariant.

Body has no separate identity criterion. Entity supplies persistent identity; Body states how that Entity is embodied at the indexed States within the declared Scope. One Body Relation may therefore relate the same Entity across changing constituent Configurations. The Entity's Boundary distinguishes its Interior from its Environment without requiring isolation.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-D2 type=proposed_definition -->
**EC-D2 — Bodily Organization:** Bodily Organization is a Relation among at least two distinct Organs related as constituents by one Body at named States. Each Organ has a distinct recurring Transformation inside the Entity's Interior and an exact Causal Contribution whose downstream Change preserves the Entity's identity Invariant. At least one such downstream State feeds a later recurring Transformation of another named Organ under the shared Direction. Shared enclosure, dependence, Substrate, proximity, or parallel contribution to Persistence alone does not establish Bodily Organization.

The exact inter-Organ feed replaces an unconstrained coordination predicate. Constituent membership is witnessed through the Body Relation rather than inferred from enclosure.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-D3 type=proposed_definition -->
**EC-D3 — Embodied Perspective:** An Embodied Perspective is a Model within an Entity's Interior under the Scope and Specification of one Body Relation. One Representation denotes the Entity's own condition, and every advertised available internal Transformation has its own Representation and exact Denotation to that Transformation's output. A Difference in the condition Representation registered through Perception and a Difference supplied by Memory each form the named upstream Difference of a Causal Contribution to a later State in the same Interior under stated Constraints. Verbal self-description is not required.

“Operative” is carried by exact equalities between the representational Differences and the contributions' upstream Differences. Merely storing a Representation beside a causal mechanism cannot satisfy the structure.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-D4 type=proposed_definition -->
**EC-D4 — Recurrent Integration:** Recurrent Integration is an organization of at least two distinct named nonempty families of constituent Transformations under one Direction. The first Causal Contribution's two comparison paths begin with Transformations in the first family and its downstream Change belongs to the second family; the return contribution's comparison paths begin in the second family and its downstream Change belongs to the first. The return contribution begins later than the first downstream Change. Shared storage, broadcast, duplicated signals, temporal co-occurrence, or correlation alone does not establish Recurrent Integration.

The two Causal Contributions supply their own contrastive upstream Differences. Exact membership replaces unconstrained process-registration and process-affection predicates.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-D5 type=proposed_definition -->
**EC-D5 — Internal Activity Selection:** Internal Activity Selection is a Configuration in which the Difference between a Representation of an outcome and an admissible contrast Representation is the named upstream Difference of a Causal Contribution that discriminates among at least two available Transformations related by one Body. The outcome Representation denotes the selected Transformation's output, the contribution's downstream output equals that output, and both alternatives occur within the Entity's Interior under the Body Relation's Scope and Specification.

This term is required because binding Action crosses an Entity's Boundary. Internal Activity Selection can occur wholly within an Interior and therefore cannot be defined as Action or Agency. When it later produces a boundary-crossing Transformation, the existing Action and Agency definitions apply without modification.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-D6 type=proposed_definition -->
**EC-D6 — Embodied recurrent consciousness candidate:** One proposal-local candidate condition for consciousness obtains exactly when Body, Embodied Perspective, Recurrent Integration, and Internal Activity Selection share the same Entity, Body Relation, Scope, Interior Specification, Direction, and causal-path interpretation; every Transformation and Change in the recurrent families belongs to that Body Relation; and the perspective's condition Representation and exact Causal Contribution are the Representation and contribution that perform the selection or revision.

The complete structural conjunction supplies the semantics of the existing `CandidateCondition` protocol; no separately authored obtainment predicate remains. It does not become `organon:Consciousness`, and its obtainment remains distinct from Consciousness Attribution and Consciousness Designation.

## Exact anti-entailments

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-C1 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**EC-C1 — A Body Relation does not entail a fixed inventory of parts:** One finite Body Relation joins the same Entity to two ordered internal States while a named sensor part is present in the first and absent in the second. The Entity's identity Invariant holds at both States; no Body identity is posited.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-C2 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**EC-C2 — Boundary does not entail isolation:** The finite Body has disjoint Interior and Environment Scopes and an inhabited Causal path whose first Transformation starts in the Environment and ends in the Interior.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-C3 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**EC-C3 — Shared enclosure does not entail Bodily Organization:** A finite Body relates two distinct parts as constituents but contains only one recurring Transformation. No `BodilyOrganization` can inhabit it because that structure requires distinct recurring Transformations joined across Organs.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-C4 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**EC-C4 — Shared signals do not entail Recurrent Integration:** A finite structure gives two distinct processes the same signal. Under a Direction admitting no Transformation, no Causal Contribution and therefore no Recurrent Integration can exist.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-C5 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**EC-C5 — Embodied Perspective does not entail the candidate condition:** The finite model contains an Embodied Perspective at a named moment in a one-Transformation Body. No `InternalActivitySelection`, and therefore no complete embodied recurrent structure, can inhabit that same Body and moment.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-C6 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**EC-C6 — The complete embodied structure does not entail speech, outward Action, or complete Control:** This anti-entailment remains binding prose but lies outside the present Lean boundary. The core formal reduct has no canonical structure for speech, Action, or complete Control whose negation could be witnessed without inventing a vacuous local predicate.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-C7 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**EC-C7 — The complete embodied structure does not entail Attribution or Designation:** This anti-entailment remains binding prose but lies outside the present Lean boundary. The existing consciousness structures specify positive Attribution and Designation witnesses; the reduct does not provide closed-world negations from which their absence could be proved.

## Formal shadow

The noncanonical [EmbodiedConsciousness.lean](../ontology/formal/EmbodiedConsciousness.lean) defines `Body`, `BodilyOrganization`, `EmbodiedPerspective`, `TransformationFamily`, `RecurrentIntegration`, `InternalActivitySelection`, `EmbodiedRecurrentStructure`, and `EmbodiedRecurrentAt`. It imports the binding formal reduct's `Entity`, `Boundary`, `PersistenceWitness`, `CausalContribution`, `Denotation`, `Scope`, and `Specification` structures and reuses the existing consciousness proposal's `CandidateCondition` rather than adding a universal consciousness predicate.

The inhabited model has six ordered internal States, an external State, one identity Invariant, one Boundary Constraint, six recurring internal Transformations, two constituent Organs, two distinct nonempty Transformation families, two temporally ordered Causal Contributions forming the return path, an Embodied Perspective, a discriminating revision among internal Transformations, and one obtaining candidate. `candidateHoldsIffEmbodiedRecurrentAt` proves that candidate obtainment is exactly structural inhabitation.

Five finite witnesses test the formal boundary: changing constituents under one Body Relation, an actual Environment-to-Interior path, shared enclosure with no `BodilyOrganization`, a shared signal with no possible `RecurrentIntegration`, and an Embodied Perspective at a named moment with no complete embodied recurrent structure. EC-C6 and EC-C7 are explicitly outside the Lean boundary rather than represented by invented always-false predicates.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-G1 type=open_formalization_gate -->
### EC-G1 — Open formalization gate: constituent and process identity

Organon v0.19 binds the Body Relation only within named States and Scope, and Recurrent Integration individuates its constituents only as named Transformation families. How an Organ is reidentified across replacement of parts and whether a universal mereology or process ontology should exist remain open. The formal shadow carries explicit witnesses without claiming either theory.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement EC-G2 type=open_evidence_gate -->
### EC-G2 — Open evidence gate: candidate application

No observation in this dossier establishes that any biological, artificial, institutional, or other Entity satisfies EC-D6. Application requires a separate Specification of the relevant Entity, Body, processes, Causal Contributions, and admissible Evidence. Candidate obtainment, Attribution, Designation, Standing, protection, and moral status remain separate Claims.

## Promotion boundary

Organon v0.19 promotes Body, Bodily Organization, Embodied Perspective, Recurrent Integration, and Internal Activity Selection together with C32 through C38. Five anti-entailments have finite Lean witnesses; EC-C6 and EC-C7 state explicit limits outside the current formal boundary. The promoted constraints refer to the complete structural conjunction rather than making the quarantined candidate half-binding.

The underlying consciousness condition remains quarantined. EC-D6 remains only a proposal-local candidate available to Consciousness Attribution and Consciousness Designation. Its application still requires the evidence gate in EC-G2, while v0.19 records the bounded constituent and process-identity limits from EC-G1 in binding prose.
