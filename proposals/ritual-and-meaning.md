# Proposal: Ritual and ritual-dependent Meaning

## Verdict

Promote Ritual and Meaning as derived terms, strengthen Flow so it supplies the recurrence mechanism they require, and introduce no primitive or foundational axiom. Do not promote Recurrence or Ritual Residue: Flow already names repeated Transformation, while Record, Memory, Change, Consequence, Environment, and Causal Contribution already type the persistent effects left by Ritual.

The candidate commitment is narrower than “ritual makes everything meaningful.” Meaning is the participant-indexed Relation constituted and sustained through Ritual. A target does not contain Meaning in isolation, repetition without participant-bound causal access is only Flow, and a dormant remnant is not a reservoir of Meaning. Private Ritual is sufficient. Social propagation can produce a causally derived Meaning Relation, but not numerical identity with the source Relation.

## Evidence and adopted clarification

The immediate evidence is Daniel's adopted clarification: recurrence matters only when repeatedly joined to a participant; a private practice qualifies; the Relation sustained by that enactment is the Meaning; visible cessation need not end it instantly while actual derived effects continue contributing; when both enactment and those contributions stop, the current Relation is no longer sustained. The Sun example supplies the boundary case: recurrent solar Transformation is a Flow, while its repeated causal entry into a participant through Perception can compose a Ritual.

This clarification does not prove a universal theory of meaning. It fixes the binding use of the new Organon term and leaves semantic Denotation, truth conditions, Beauty, Play, Love, goodness, consciousness, moral worth, and generic Value distinct.

## Termhood challenge

### Rejected new term: Recurrence

The strongest reduction is successful. Flow already names repeated similar Transformations along persistent Relations. Its old definition leaves similarity and repetition under-specified, so this proposal repairs Flow by requiring at least two distinct occurrences, a shared Direction, a constructive Rule and Specification of similarity, a Scope, and a named persistent Relation or Invariant. A second Recurrence term would duplicate that structure.

### Ritual survives reduction to Flow

Flow alone can occur with no participant. Ritual adds what Flow lacks: participant-bound Causal paths, repeated causal entry into Perception, Interpretation, or Action, and Persistence of one participant-indexed Relation through those joined effects. Removing those joins classifies sunrise, an unattended oscillator, and a repeating cron job as Ritual merely because they recur.

### Rejected new term: Ritual Residue

The strongest reduction is also successful. A ritual-derived effect may already be a Record, Memory, Change in an Entity or Environment, Consequence, or Configuration of these terms. Causal Contribution states whether that effect actually makes a later Difference. Naming the collection Ritual Residue would add no classification not already available. The proposal therefore uses “ritual-derived effect” descriptively and denies that such an effect contains Meaning at rest.

### Meaning survives reduction to Preference and Denotation

Preference orders candidate States or Consequences; Meaning need not order anything. Denotation joins an expression to a target; Meaning need not be representational, and a Denotation can persist without ritual participation. Meaning is instead the participant-indexed Relation whose obtainment and Persistence are constituted by a Ritual. Eliminating Meaning loses the distinction between the recurrent Configuration and the Relation it sustains.

## Dependency-ordered statements

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-D1 type=proposed_definition -->
**RM-D1 — Flow:** Flow is a Configuration containing at least two distinct Transformation occurrences ordered under one Direction and within one Scope, where one Rule and constructive Specification decide the respect in which the occurrences are similar and a named Relation or Invariant persists across their ordered States. The States need not repeat identically, the intervals need not be periodic, and the Entity producing the Classification need not be the source of the Transformations.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-D2 type=proposed_definition -->
**RM-D2 — Ritual:** Ritual is a persistent Configuration in which a Flow is repeatedly joined through participant-bound Causal paths to the Perception, Interpretation, or Action of at least one participating Entity, and Causal Contribution from those joined occurrences preserves one named participant-indexed Relation across distinct participant States within a Scope. One participating Entity is sufficient. The recurrent target may be an Entity, State, Relation, Configuration, or environmental Difference and need not itself be an Agent, but it must occupy the declared target position in every qualifying Flow occurrence and the sustaining Causal Contribution rather than enter through Scope membership alone.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-D3 type=proposed_definition -->
**RM-D3 — Meaning:** Meaning is the participant-indexed Relation constituted and sustained by a Ritual among its participating Entities and target Presences within a Scope. Its Persistence requires ongoing Ritual enactment or actual Causal Contribution from ritual-derived Records, Memories, Changes, Consequences, or environmental Configurations; merely preserving one of those possible carriers does not preserve Meaning by itself.

## Exact entailments and anti-entailments

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C1 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C1 — Flow and access do not separately entail Ritual:** Ritual entails Flow and at least one participant-bound causal join. Flow alone does not entail Ritual. One Perception, Interpretation, or Action does not entail Ritual without a qualifying Flow and Persistence of the participant-indexed Relation. Ritual does not require multiple participants, an Institution, an inherited social form, periodic intervals, or an Agent as its target.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C2 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C2 — Meaning is maintained, not stored:** Meaning entails a sustaining Ritual, while a Ritual entails only the Relation it actually sustains in its declared Scope. A target Presence, Record, Memory, Symbol, shrine, or other ritual-derived Configuration does not contain Meaning independently. Ending visible enactment does not by itself end Meaning while actual Causal Contributions derived from the Ritual continue preserving the Relation. When neither enactment nor any such contribution preserves it, the Relation ceases to obtain in the continuing Scope; this does not erase its former Presence or Records of it.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C3 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C3 — Propagation is derivation, not copying:** When one Ritual affects another participant through a Causal Contribution and another Meaning Relation obtains, the later Relation is causally derived but not numerically identical to the first because its participant index differs. Shared target, Representation, Rule, or form of enactment does not make the Relations one.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C4 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C4 — Significance does not silently become valuation or status:** Ritual and Meaning do not entail Preference, Utility Measure, Price, institutional valuation, Truth, consent, goodness, consciousness, moral status, moral personhood, or moral worth. None of those terms entails Ritual or Meaning without the required Flow, participant-bound causal joins, and sustaining Relation. Beauty, Play, and Love remain ordinary or quarantined vocabulary rather than aliases for Meaning.

## Intellectual shadows

Rappaport's account of ritual as formalized, repeated performance supplies the strongest local shadow for recurrence and enactment. Catherine Bell's ritualization resists treating Ritual as a neutral inventory of ceremonial objects. Durkheim and Collins emphasize collective participation and transmitted social effects; Organon deliberately permits one participant and treats later social effects as causally derived Relations rather than a group prerequisite. Peirce and Wittgenstein discipline the separation between signification, interpretive use, and practice; Organon retains Denotation and Rule-governed use as distinct from ritual-dependent Meaning.

The Shinto shrine analogy motivates rather than proves the account: a Presence can become the target of Meaning while recurrent veneration sustains the participant-indexed Relation. Organon does not claim that Shinto practice reduces to this schema or that every shrine's significance has one Rule.

The proposed contribution is the unification: recurring performance, participant access, Causal Contribution, Persistence, and a non-transferable Relation must inhabit the same dependency chain. It is not the discovery that rituals repeat, that practices carry significance, or that social effects propagate.

## Formal shadow

The noncanonical [RitualMeaning.lean](../ontology/formal/RitualMeaning.lean) strengthens Flow with a typed `FlowRule` that owns the exact executable Specification used for every occurrence, two distinct Transformation occurrences, and a named Persistence witness. It constructs one private participant Entity whose ordered history contains the endpoints of two participant-bound access paths. A Ritual requires access for every Flow occurrence and requires the same typed target component at every occurrence output. Meaning carries that exact participant-target pair and a canonical paired-path Causal Contribution: both compared paths begin with exact Flow occurrences, so the theorem `meaningContributionCarriesRitualTarget` derives the target at both causal origins, while the selected endpoint is proved equal to the participant's current State.

The finite model supplies a bare Flow value carrying no participant access, a single access path carrying no repeated Flow, a private Ritual and Meaning, a target distinct from its Meaning participant, a derived-effect maintenance classifier, a dormant-effect failure classifier, and a propagation witness whose same Causal Contribution reaches distinct source and recipient participant States and whose Meaning Relations are proved unequal. The first two are structural insufficiency examples, not logical countermodels to C25: because canonical Perception and uptake are absent from Lean, RM-G1 blocks that stronger theorem.

The model does not pretend that an unlabeled endpoint in an Entity history is already a complete Sense-to-Perception witness. It also does not formalize Beauty, Play, Love, goodness, consent, consciousness, moral worth, or a metric of temporal decay.

## Open gates

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-G1 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**RM-G1 — Complete participant-access parity:** The formal access path begins with the exact Flow occurrence and ends in the participant's ordered history. Full parity remains blocked until the canonical formal shadow exposes Sense, Perception, Interpretation, Action, Interior membership, and Boundary crossing sufficiently to prove the selected route rather than naming it in prose.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-G2 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**RM-G2 — Temporal maintenance and decay:** The formal classifier proves that an actual derived effect can maintain the relation when visible enactment is inactive and that neither source leaves it maintained. It does not define elapsed time, decay rate, thresholds, revival, or continuity across arbitrarily long gaps. Every such Claim must declare its temporal Scope and sustaining path.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-G3 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**RM-G3 — Qualitative descendants:** Beauty, Play, Love, sacredness, grief, goodness, consent, moral worth, and consciousness remain outside this promotion. Future proposals must decide whether Ritual generates, expresses, stabilizes, transmits, or merely accompanies each candidate condition without treating Meaning as a universal bridge.

## Promotion decision

Promote RM-D1 through RM-D3 and RM-C1 through RM-C4. Keep RM-G1 through RM-G3 open. Flow is revised in place with stable identity because its extension remains repeated similar Transformation and the revision supplies previously implicit classification witnesses. Recurrence and Ritual Residue receive no stable identifiers.
