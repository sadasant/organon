# Proposal: Ritual and ritual-dependent Meaning

## Verdict

Promote Ritual and Meaning as derived terms, strengthen Flow by making its previously implicit recurrence Relation explicit, and introduce no primitive or foundational axiom. Do not make constructive classification constitutive of Flow: place its Rule and Specification in Flow Claims and in Ritual's participant-relative Interpretation. Do not promote Recurrence or Ritual Residue: Flow already names repeated Transformation, while Record, Memory, Change, Consequence, Environment, and Causal Contribution already type the persistent effects left by Ritual.

The candidate commitment is narrower than “ritual makes everything meaningful.” Meaning is the participant-indexed Relation constituted and sustained through Ritual. A target does not contain Meaning in isolation, repetition without memory-conditioned Interpretation is only Flow or conditioning, and a dormant remnant is not a reservoir of Meaning. Private Ritual is sufficient. Social propagation can produce a causally derived Meaning Relation, but not numerical identity with the source Relation.

The account is also normatively neutral. Ritual need not be chosen, beneficial, or benign. Addiction and trauma loops can satisfy it when recurrence enters Perception, load-bearing Memory conditions later Interpretation, and the resulting Causal Contribution sustains the participant-indexed Relation. Repetition, compulsion, distress, or diagnosis alone is not enough. The “repeated too often, becomes a trap” case is therefore included without treating pathology as the genus of Ritual or Meaning as endorsement.

## Evidence and adopted clarification

The immediate evidence is Daniel's adopted clarification: recurrence matters only when repeatedly joined to a participant; a private practice qualifies; the Relation sustained by that enactment is the Meaning; visible cessation need not end it instantly while actual derived effects continue contributing; when both enactment and those contributions stop, the current Relation is no longer sustained. The Sun example supplies the boundary case: recurrent solar Transformation is a Flow and repeated seeing is recurrent Perception; Ritual obtains only when Memory of prior encounters causally conditions Interpretation of a later encounter and thereby sustains the participant-target Relation.

This clarification does not prove a universal theory of meaning. It fixes the binding use of the new Organon term and leaves semantic Denotation, truth conditions, Beauty, Play, Love, goodness, consciousness, moral worth, and generic Value distinct.

## Termhood challenge

### Rejected new term: Recurrence

The strongest reduction is successful. Flow already names repeated similar Transformations along persistent Relations. Its old definition leaves similarity and repetition under-specified, so this proposal repairs Flow by requiring at least two distinct occurrences, one object-level recurrence Relation, a shared Direction and Scope, and a named persistent Relation or Invariant. A second Recurrence term would duplicate that structure.

An earlier draft also made one constructive Rule and Specification constitutive of Flow. That was rejected because it changed an ontic pattern into a representation-dependent classification: an unclassified recurrence would cease to be Flow, and downstream Institution and Center would inherit an unintended decidability requirement. The binding revision instead requires every reproducible Flow Claim to name the recurrence Relation and carry a constructive classifier. Ritual retains the stronger requirement because participant Interpretation must recognize the later occurrence as recurrence.

### Ritual survives reduction to Flow

Flow alone can occur with no participant. Ritual adds what Flow lacks: participant-bound Perception, an internal Memory of a prior occurrence that makes a contrastive Difference to later Interpretation, and a sustaining Causal Contribution passing through that Interpretation. Removing those joins classifies sunrise, an unattended oscillator, repeated exposure without retention, and conditioning without Interpretation as Ritual merely because they recur.

### Rejected new term: Ritual Residue

The strongest reduction is also successful. A ritual-derived effect may already be a Record, Memory, Change in an Entity or Environment, Consequence, or Configuration of these terms. Causal Contribution states whether that effect actually makes a later Difference. Naming the collection Ritual Residue would add no classification not already available. The proposal therefore uses “ritual-derived effect” descriptively and denies that such an effect contains Meaning at rest.

### Meaning survives reduction to Preference and Denotation

Preference orders candidate States or Consequences; Meaning need not order anything. Denotation joins an expression to a target; Meaning need not be representational, and a Denotation can persist without ritual participation. Meaning is instead the participant-indexed Relation whose obtainment and Persistence are constituted by a Ritual. Eliminating Meaning loses the distinction between the recurrent Configuration and the Relation it sustains.

## Dependency-ordered statements

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-D1 type=proposed_definition -->
**RM-D1 — Flow:** Flow is a Configuration containing at least two distinct Transformation occurrences joined by one recurrence Relation, with their outputs ordered under one Direction within one Scope and a named Relation or Invariant persisting across those States. The recurrence Relation identifies the respect in which the occurrences repeat. The States need not be identical, the intervals need not be periodic, and no Agent, Representation, Rule, or Specification is required for the Flow to obtain. A reproducible Flow Claim must name that Relation, Scope, and Persistence witness and supply a Rule with a constructive Specification deciding occurrence membership under the Relation.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-D2 type=proposed_definition -->
**RM-D2 — Ritual:** Ritual is a persistent Configuration in which successive occurrences of a Flow enter the Perception of at least one participating Entity, an internal Memory of a prior occurrence causally conditions the Interpretation of a later occurrence, that Interpretation classifies the later occurrence as recurrence under one Rule and constructive Specification of the Flow's recurrence Relation, and the sustaining Causal Contribution passes through the memory-conditioned Interpretation to preserve one named participant-indexed Relation across distinct participant States within a Scope. Substituting a different admissible Memory must change the resulting Interpretation. One participating Entity is sufficient; outward Action, public performance, and conscious recall are not required. The recurrent target may be an Entity, State, Relation, Configuration, or environmental Difference and need not itself be an Agent. It may drift across States or Representations only when one declared identity criterion, Persistence witness, and target Invariant connect every qualifying occurrence to the same target history, with exact Denotation required whenever a Representation mediates access.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-D3 type=proposed_definition -->
**RM-D3 — Meaning:** Meaning is the participant-indexed Relation constituted and sustained by a Ritual among its participating Entities and target Presences within a Scope. Its Persistence requires ongoing Ritual enactment or actual Causal Contribution from ritual-derived Records, Memories, Changes, Consequences, or environmental Configurations; merely preserving one of those possible carriers does not preserve Meaning by itself.

## Exact entailments and anti-entailments

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C1 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C1 — Flow and access do not separately entail Ritual:** Ritual entails Flow, participant-relative constructive classification of its recurrence Relation, participant-bound Perception of successive occurrences, a prior Memory whose substitution changes later Interpretation, and a sustaining Causal Contribution passing through that Interpretation. Flow, a Flow classifier, recurrent exposure, a stored Record, Perception, Memory, Interpretation, or Action alone does not entail Ritual. Ritual does not require an outward Action, multiple participants, an Institution, an inherited social form, periodic intervals, conscious recall, or an Agent as its target.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C2 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C2 — Meaning is maintained, not stored:** Meaning entails a sustaining Ritual, while a Ritual entails only the Relation it actually sustains in its declared Scope. A target Presence, Record, Memory, Symbol, shrine, or other ritual-derived Configuration does not contain Meaning independently. Ending visible enactment does not by itself end Meaning while actual Causal Contributions derived from the Ritual continue preserving the Relation. When neither enactment nor any such contribution preserves it, the Relation ceases to obtain in the continuing Scope; this does not erase its former Presence or Records of it.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C3 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C3 — Propagation is derivation, not copying:** When one Ritual affects another participant through a Causal Contribution and another Meaning Relation obtains, the later Relation is causally derived but not numerically identical to the first because its participant index differs. Shared target, Representation, Rule, or form of enactment does not make the Relations one.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C4 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C4 — Significance does not silently become valuation or status:** Ritual and Meaning do not entail Preference, Utility Measure, Price, institutional valuation, Truth, consent, goodness, consciousness, moral status, moral personhood, or moral worth. None of those terms entails Ritual or Meaning without the required Flow, participant-bound causal joins, and sustaining Relation. Beauty, Play, and Love remain ordinary or quarantined vocabulary rather than aliases for Meaning.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C5 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C5 — Target drift is neither identity nor substitution by default:** Variation among target States or Representations preserves one Ritual target only when a declared identity criterion and Persistence witness preserve one target Invariant and every qualifying occurrence reaches or denotes a State in that history. Drift within the Invariant does not terminate the Ritual. Breaking the Invariant constitutes target substitution; any subsequently constituted Meaning Relation is numerically distinct though it may be causally derived. Exact State equality, resemblance, shared naming, uninterrupted enactment, or Denotation alone does not prove continuity.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C6 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C6 — Flow and its classification do not collapse:** Flow obtains through its Transformation occurrences, recurrence Relation, ordered States, and Persistence, not through the Representation that classifies it. A Rule and constructive Specification are required for a reproducible Flow Claim but neither creates the recurrence Relation nor proves that selected occurrences inhabit it. Flow may obtain without a classifier, and multiple classifiers can track one Flow without multiplying it.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-C7 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**RM-C7 — Ritual does not entail benefit or consent:** Ritual and Meaning do not entail voluntary Action, consent, Preference, benefit, health, liberation, or moral endorsement. Addiction and trauma loops may instantiate Ritual and sustain Meaning when every defining causal and interpretive join obtains; recurrence, compulsion, distress, or diagnosis alone does not establish either term.

## Intellectual shadows

Rappaport's account of ritual as formalized, repeated performance supplies the strongest local shadow for recurrence and enactment. Catherine Bell's ritualization resists treating Ritual as a neutral inventory of ceremonial objects. Durkheim and Collins emphasize collective participation and transmitted social effects; Organon deliberately permits one participant and treats later social effects as causally derived Relations rather than a group prerequisite. Peirce and Wittgenstein discipline the separation between signification, interpretive use, and practice; Organon retains Denotation and Rule-governed use as distinct from ritual-dependent Meaning.

The Shinto shrine analogy motivates rather than proves the account: a Presence can become the target of Meaning while recurrent veneration sustains the participant-indexed Relation. Organon does not claim that Shinto practice reduces to this schema or that every shrine's significance has one Rule.

The proposed contribution is the unification: recurring performance, participant access, Causal Contribution, Persistence, and a non-transferable Relation must inhabit the same dependency chain. It is not the discovery that rituals repeat, that practices carry significance, or that social effects propagate.

## Formal shadow

The noncanonical [RitualMeaning.lean](../ontology/formal/RitualMeaning.lean) gives Flow two distinct Transformation occurrences, an object-level recurrence predicate that holds across the ordered occurrence list, and a named Persistence witness whose history is exactly the output sequence. `FlowClassification` is separate: its typed `FlowRule` owns one executable Specification, the same structure joins conformity and recurrence for the selected sequence, and a named in-Scope non-occurrence is rejected. A bare Flow therefore inhabits Lean without carrying a classifier. Ritual carries one exact `FlowClassification`. `RitualUptake` proves its prior and current occurrences both conform and inhabit the Flow's recurrence Relation, retains the prior contribution endpoint in participant history, contains the later Interpretation occurrence in the sustaining path, and supplies a constructive Interpretation function whose output changes under contrastive Perception or Memory. `TargetContinuity` replaces exact target equality with an ordered target-State history carrying one Invariant; every Flow occurrence and both origins of Meaning's sustaining contribution are proved members of that history.

The finite model supplies a bare Flow value carrying no participant access, a single access path carrying no repeated Flow, a private Ritual and Meaning, contrastively load-bearing Perception and Memory, two unequal target States satisfying one target Invariant, one candidate replacement State that fails that Invariant, a derived-effect maintenance classifier, a dormant-effect failure classifier, and a propagation witness whose same Causal Contribution reaches distinct source and recipient participant States and whose Meaning Relations are proved unequal. The first two are structural insufficiency examples, not logical countermodels to C25: because canonical Perception, Memory, and Interpretation are absent from OrganonCore, RM-G1 blocks that stronger theorem.

The model does not pretend that an unlabeled endpoint in an Entity history is already a complete Sense-to-Perception witness. It also does not formalize Beauty, Play, Love, goodness, consent, consciousness, moral worth, or a metric of temporal decay.

## Open gates

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-G1 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**RM-G1 — Complete participant-uptake parity:** The formal access path begins with the exact Flow occurrence and ends in the participant's ordered history. The proposal-local `RitualUptake` constructively proves that a retained prior effect changes a later Interpretation result and that the sustaining contribution contains that Interpretation occurrence. Full parity remains blocked until the canonical formal shadow exposes Sense, Perception, Memory, Interpretation, Action, Interior membership, and Boundary crossing sufficiently to type the selected route with the binding terms rather than proposal-local State roles.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-G2 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**RM-G2 — Temporal maintenance and decay:** The formal classifier proves that an actual derived effect can maintain the relation when visible enactment is inactive and that neither source leaves it maintained. It does not define elapsed time, decay rate, thresholds, revival, or continuity across arbitrarily long gaps. Every such Claim must declare its temporal Scope and sustaining path.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-G3 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**RM-G3 — Qualitative descendants:** Beauty, Play, Love, sacredness, grief, goodness, consent, moral worth, and consciousness remain outside this promotion. Future proposals must decide whether Ritual generates, expresses, stabilizes, transmits, or merely accompanies each candidate condition without treating Meaning as a universal bridge.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement RM-G4 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**RM-G4 — Represented target continuity:** The formal witness proves direct target-State drift inside one Persistence history and Invariant. It does not yet construct changing Representations with exact Denotations to those target States. Any Classification of continuity through icons, names, rebuilt artifacts, or inherited forms remains blocked until those Denotation joins are explicit.

## Promotion decision

Promote RM-D1 through RM-D3 and RM-C1 through RM-C7. Keep RM-G1 through RM-G4 open. Flow is revised in place with stable identity because its extension remains repeated similar Transformation and the revision makes its previously implicit recurrence Relation explicit without requiring a classifier for ontic obtainment. The rejected draft that made Specification constitutive was never canonical and requires no migration. Recurrence and Ritual Residue receive no stable identifiers.
