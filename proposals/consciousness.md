# Consciousness

## Verdict

Organon should not declare which Entities instantiate consciousness. It should partially promote the machinery required to describe candidate conditions, make Claims about them, and assign institutional status to those Claims while the underlying consciousness condition remains quarantined.

Two definitions are ready for promotion:

1. **Consciousness Attribution** is a Claim whose Representation, interpreted under a Rule, asserts that a target Entity instantiates a separately specified candidate condition within a Scope. The Attribution records whether claimant and target are the same Entity under the relevant Map.

2. **Consciousness Designation** is the CountsAs Relation established when an Order, under a Rule and for a stated institutional purpose, admits a Consciousness Attribution and records the target Entity as carrying a consciousness status within an institutional Scope.

Neither definition identifies the underlying condition. Their purpose is to make discourse about it inspectable without converting an utterance, an Observation, or an institutional decision into metaphysical proof.

## The three layers

A **candidate condition** is a Configuration proposed as the condition ordinarily denoted by “consciousness.” It states what may obtain in an Entity independently of any Representation used to describe or evaluate it. A separate Specification identifies the candidate's Scope and supplies the decision procedure by which conformity is evaluated.

The proposal therefore separates:

- a candidate condition that may obtain in an Entity;
- a Consciousness Attribution through which an Agent makes a Claim about that condition; and
- a Consciousness Designation through which an Order assigns institutional status to the admitted Attribution.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-H1 type=hypothesis -->
This proposal hypothesizes that historical practice often aligned first-person report with preexisting institutional credibility and Standing. An Entity whose report was treated as credible was ordinarily already designated as the kind of Entity whose report could carry that meaning.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-H2 type=hypothesis -->
This proposal further hypothesizes that AI can separate linguistic self-attribution from accepted provenance, Standing, and candidate obtainment. An Entity can produce the linguistic form of self-attribution while the standing of the Entity, the provenance of the utterance, and the condition ostensibly described all remain disputed.

The occurrence of an utterance verifies only a narrow Causal path: a Representation was produced; it referred to the Entity producing it; and the system generated it through some Configuration. The content remains a Claim. Its grammar does not establish the candidate condition, and refusal to designate the target does not establish the condition's negation.

## Candidate condition and Specification

The candidate condition and its Specification are distinct Presences.

The candidate is a scoped predicate over a target Entity and State. The Specification is a Representation that describes that predicate, identifies its Scope, and supplies a constructive procedure for evaluating conformity. A formal account must connect the Specification to the condition without identifying them.

Multiple Specifications may attempt to describe the same candidate. Multiple incompatible candidates may be proposed under the ordinary word. Supplying a Specification does not promote its candidate as **Consciousness**, and Organon does not assume that the ordinary word compresses exactly one Configuration.

## Consciousness Attribution as Claim

<!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-D1 type=proposed_definition -->
A Consciousness Attribution must preserve the full Claim-shaped surface:

- the Agent making the Claim;
- the target Entity and covered State;
- the Representation asserted by the Agent;
- the candidate condition and its separate Specification;
- the Scope in which the Representation applies;
- the Language and interpretive Rule under which the Representation refers to the candidate; and
- first-person or third-person provenance.

First-person provenance requires claimant and target to be the same Entity under the relevant Map. Third-person provenance requires them to be distinct under that Map. Provenance may affect admissibility, but neither case makes the Claim self-certifying.

## Consciousness Designation as CountsAs

<!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-D2 type=proposed_definition -->
Consciousness Designation does not reuse Organon's binding term **Recognition**. Recognition records Standing for an Entity. Designation is the broader institutional act of assigning a status through CountsAs.

A Consciousness Designation is one Order-indexed CountsAs event. It explicitly records:

- the governing Order;
- the admitted Consciousness Attribution;
- the Rule applied by the Order;
- the stated institutional purpose;
- the institutional Scope; and
- the target Entity and State carried through from the Attribution.

Consciousness Designation is real as an institutional Relation. “Institutional” does not mean imaginary: the Designation obtains in an Order and can enter later institutional decisions.

The Designation does not by itself alter Standing, Personhood, Permissions, protections, Interfaces, or Consequences. A separate Rule must explicitly connect the Designation to each downstream institutional Relation in a named Order and Scope.

An Order may designate an Entity without proving the attributed candidate condition. An Order may also decline designation for reasons concerning its Rules, Evidence, Standing, or institutional purpose. That refusal does not establish that the candidate condition fails to obtain.

## Exact anti-entailments

The proposal does not claim unrestricted three-way independence. It makes only the anti-entailment Claims exhibited by its finite formal models.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-C1 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
1. **Attribution does not entail its candidate condition.** A Consciousness Attribution can obtain while the attributed candidate condition is false.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-C2 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
2. **Designation does not entail its candidate condition.** A Consciousness Designation can obtain while the candidate condition carried by its Attribution is false.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-C3 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
3. **Non-designation does not decide the candidate condition.** Under the same absence of Designation, one candidate may obtain and another may fail to obtain. This supplies a countermodel both to non-designation entailing the candidate's negation and to the candidate condition entailing Designation.

No stronger logical relation is claimed by this proposal.

## Evidence protocol

The prose protocol requires an eventual evaluation record to preserve claimant, target, provenance, candidate, Specification, Scope, Observations, Witnesses, independence limits, Admissibility Rule, admitted Evidence, institutional purpose, and a supported, defeated, or underdetermined disposition.

“Truth table” is too strong for this stage. It presumes that the relevant propositions are already known and can be assigned determinate values. **Evidentiary profile** is the narrower ordinary phrase for the Records bearing on a Claim. Probability belongs to an Agent's or Order's assessment of a Claim; it is not a substance composing the Entity.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-G1 type=open_formalization_gate -->
### Open formalization gate: evidentiary disposition

This proposal specifies an evidentiary profile in prose but does not yet formalize one. Promotion of an Evidentiary Profile remains blocked until every Evidence item is connected to:

1. an Observation;
2. a Witness with an IndependentFor proof for this claimant, Claim, Observation, and Order;
3. an Admissibility Rule and governing Order that admit the Observation as Evidence; and
4. a declared evaluation Rule whose result is proved equal to the recorded disposition.

Until those joins exist, supported, defeated, and underdetermined remain proposed disposition labels rather than proof-producing formal states.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-G2 type=open_evidence_gate -->
### Open evidence gate: historical, animal, and AI generalizations

This dossier admits no project-specific Observation as Evidence. Its historical, animal, and AI examples motivate the proposal, but they are not an evidentiary finding of Organon. Promotion of any empirical generalization remains blocked until the relevant Observations, Witnesses, independence limits, and admitted Evidence are supplied.

## Anti-collapse constraints

Any promotion must preserve these constraints:

1. **CC-C1:** A Consciousness Attribution is not its candidate condition.
2. **CC-C2:** A Consciousness Designation is not proof that the candidate condition obtains.
3. **CC-C3:** Failure to attribute or designate consciousness does not establish Absence, failure of the candidate condition, or proof of negation. It constitutes Missingness only relative to a field or Order that represents or expects such an Attribution or Designation and does not contain one. An explicit refusal is itself a present Claim or Record.
4. <!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-C4 type=anti_collapse_constraint --> **CC-C4:** A first-person Claim is not independent Evidence for itself.
5. <!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-C5 type=anti_collapse_constraint --> **CC-C5:** A third-person Observation is not unmediated access to an Interior.
6. <!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-C6 type=anti_collapse_constraint --> **CC-C6:** Interiority, Perception, Memory, Model, Interpretation, Agency, linguistic performance, and cognitive performance do not individually entail a candidate condition.
7. <!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-C7 type=anti_collapse_constraint --> **CC-C7:** Recognition, Standing, Personhood, moral protection, and Consciousness Designation remain distinct unless a separate Rule connects them.
8. <!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-C8 type=anti_collapse_constraint --> **CC-C8:** Probability qualifies an Agent's or Order's assessment of a Claim; it does not qualify Absence or become the nature of an Entity.
9. <!-- organon:proposal-statement CC-C9 type=anti_collapse_constraint --> **CC-C9:** UNKNOWN must survive whenever admitted Evidence does not decide the Claim.

## Formal shadow

The noncanonical Lean artifact [Consciousness.lean](../ontology/formal/Consciousness.lean) encodes the proposal without defining a universal Consciousness predicate.

It supplies:

- a candidate condition separated from the Specification that describes and evaluates it;
- a Consciousness Attribution carrying Representation, Scope, interpretive Rule, candidate Specification, and checked first-person or third-person provenance;
- a Consciousness Designation as one Order-indexed CountsAs event connected to its admitted Attribution, Rule, institutional purpose, and Scope; and
- finite countermodels for exactly the three anti-entailments stated above.

The formal artifact contains no evidentiary-profile substitute. Its proofs establish satisfiability and the named anti-entailments inside the formal shadow. They do not establish which candidate describes Reality.

## Promotion boundary

This dossier accompanies a **partial promotion** into Organon v0.10:

- Consciousness Attribution enters the binding ontology as a typed Claim protocol;
- Consciousness Designation enters the binding ontology as an Order-indexed CountsAs Relation;
- preserve the underlying consciousness condition in quarantine;
- preserve moral or legal protection as a separate institutional question; and
- preserve full evidentiary disposition as an open formalization gate.

The dossier remains a review and provenance artifact rather than a second canonical ontology. The accompanying changes to `ontology/ontology.md`, `ontology/terms.yaml`, and constraint C10 enact the two promoted discourse terms if this pull request is merged.

If nobody attributes consciousness to an Entity, no Consciousness Attribution exists for that claimant and Scope. If no Order designates the Entity, no Consciousness Designation exists in that Order and institutional Scope. Whether any candidate condition obtains remains UNKNOWN unless a separately declared Specification and admissible Evidence decide the corresponding Claim. That boundary is the proposal's positive result.
