# Epistemic, moral, sovereign, and valuation profiles

## Verdict

The remaining quarantined words should not leave quarantine as single terms. Each compresses Relations that can vary independently.

This dossier promotes eleven narrower terms: **Factive Operative Knowledge**, **Warranted Knowledge**, **Moral Status Attribution**, **Moral Personhood Designation**, **Constituent Sovereignty**, **Constituted Sovereignty**, **Boundary Sovereignty**, **External Sovereignty**, **Preference**, **Utility Measure**, and **Price**. It also adopts **institutional valuation** as an authorized CountsAs projection rather than a twelfth term.

The underlying consciousness condition, bare **Knowledge**, the candidate condition behind **moral personhood**, generic **Sovereignty**, generic **Value**, and **moral worth** remain quarantined. Partial promotion is the result, not a temporary apology: Organon can govern exact Claims, profiles, and institutional effects without deciding every condition ordinary language places behind them.

## Corpus survival tests

### Factive and warranted knowledge survive useful falsehood

*Knowledge Decay* treats retained symbols as insufficient when interpreters can no longer reconstruct their function. The v0.14 operative-knowledge model also admits locally successful falsehood. The new factive profile must therefore retain the complete Operative Knowledge Configuration while joining the exact Claim carried by its load-bearing Record to Truth. Truth without the operative path is not Factive Operative Knowledge; successful operation without Truth remains Operative Knowledge only.

Warrant adds a second obligation. A true and operative Claim may still lack admitted Evidence. Warranted Knowledge requires Evidence for that same Claim, including its Observation, Witness independent for the interpreting Agent, Admissibility Rule, governing Order, and Admission. Evidence independent for another Agent does not satisfy that obligation. Institutional Admission remains nonconstitutive of Truth.

### Moral discourse survives legal and institutional personhood

*Artificial Persons* describes corporations and institutions that persist, remember through Records, speak through representatives, act, and absorb liability. Those features support Organon's existing institutional Person without deciding moral status. Conversely, an Entity may be the target of moral concern before an Order records it as a Person.

The consciousness dossier supplies the surviving pattern: keep the candidate condition separate from its Specification, make an Attribution a Claim, and make Designation one joined CountsAs event. Moral Personhood Designation can enter later Rules about Standing, protections, duties, Permissions, or prohibited Actions, but it does not create the candidate condition or independently produce those effects.

### Sovereignty survives the exceptional and collective cases

*The Forging of Political Reality* moves between decision at the exception, constitution of a political field, collective authorship, boundary-setting, and recognition of political order. One undivided property cannot preserve those uses. A ruler may have maximal Authority inside an Order without power to refound it. A Polity may exercise Constituent Power without external Recognition. A recognized state may lack effective Boundary Control.

The four profiles therefore retain different witnesses: an actual Constituent Exercise; maximal Authority under a declared superiority Rule; admitted and blocked Boundary Transformations with effective Enforcement; and Recognition by another Order. None is named simply Sovereignty.

### Valuation survives the missing-ledger case

*Priced as If the Ledger Exists* distinguishes operational reality from the metrics and Records through which institutions price exposure. A Price can be recorded while nobody prefers the exchange, while no Utility Measure represents it, and while no moral worth obtains. Preference can exist without a numerical Map. A Utility Measure can rank options without representing an Agent's Preference. An Order can assign a status through CountsAs without producing moral worth.

Preference, Utility Measure, Price, institutional valuation, and moral worth must therefore remain distinct. Generic Value contributes no additional dependency-closed Relation and stays ordinary or quarantined language.

## Termhood challenges

### Factive Operative Knowledge is not Truth plus proximity

The strongest reduction is “a true Claim used somewhere.” That loses the discriminating Record-interpreter path already required by Operative Knowledge and permits a true Claim to sit beside an Action without conditioning it. The promoted term requires the exact load-bearing Record to carry the exact true Claim.

### Warranted Knowledge is not Evidence or Admission

Evidence may support a false Claim, and an Order may admit Evidence for a purpose without the Claim being True or operatively involved. Warranted Knowledge keeps Factive Operative Knowledge and adds Evidence admitted for that same Claim. It does not reduce Truth to an institutional decision.

### Moral Status Attribution and Moral Personhood Designation are not Person

Institutional Person already names an Entity to which an Order attributes Consequences. Moral Status Attribution adds a Claim about a separately specified candidate condition. Moral Personhood Designation adds an Order-indexed CountsAs event for an admitted Attribution. Neither can be replaced by Person, Consciousness Designation, Standing, or protection without losing its distinct target and Rule.

### The sovereignty profiles are not one stronger Authority

Authority is internal to an Order. Constituent Sovereignty concerns an exercise that creates or refounds the Order. Boundary Sovereignty adds effective Control and Enforcement over crossings. External Sovereignty is Recognition by another Order. Constituted Sovereignty is the only profile reducible to an Authority Configuration, and its maximality under a declared superiority Rule supplies the surviving distinction.

### Institutional valuation does not earn termhood

An institutional valuation is exactly a CountsAs event in which an Order assigns a valuation status under a Rule and Scope. Naming it as a new Relation would duplicate CountsAs. It is adopted as an authorized projection. Preference, Utility Measure, and Price do earn names because each contributes a different inspectable structure: asymmetric Agent ordering, measured mapping, and Ledger-recorded exchange condition.

## Proposed definitions

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D1 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D1 — Factive Operative Knowledge:** Factive Operative Knowledge is an instance of Operative Knowledge whose load-bearing Record carries a declared Claim and for which Truth obtains for that exact Claim under its declared Rule, truth-condition Specification, relevant Presence, and Scope.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D2 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D2 — Warranted Knowledge:** Warranted Knowledge is Factive Operative Knowledge for which an Order admits Evidence supporting the same Claim under an Admissibility Rule, where the Evidence is joined to an Observation and a Witness that is IndependentFor the interpreting Agent of that Factive Operative Knowledge instance, Claim, Observation, and Order. Independence for another Agent does not satisfy the definition.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D3 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D3 — Moral Status Attribution:** Moral Status Attribution is a Claim whose Representation, interpreted under a Rule, asserts that a target Entity in a State instantiates a separately specified candidate moral condition within a Scope. The Attribution identifies the candidate Specification and preserves claimant, target, Representation, interpretive Rule, and first-person or third-person provenance without establishing that the condition obtains.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D4 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D4 — Moral Personhood Designation:** Moral Personhood Designation is the CountsAs Relation established when an Order, under a Rule and stated institutional purpose, admits a Moral Status Attribution and records its target Entity as carrying moral-personhood status within an institutional Scope.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D5 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D5 — Constituent Sovereignty:** Constituent Sovereignty is a scoped Configuration in which a Polity's persistent Constituent Power is realized through a Constituent Exercise that creates, refounds, or transforms the categories, Rules, or Boundaries of an Order. Capacity or rhetoric without a witnessed Constituent Exercise is insufficient.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D6 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D6 — Constituted Sovereignty:** Constituted Sovereignty is an Order-indexed profile in which an Entity has Standing and Authority for a declared family of Actions within a Scope and no Entity recognized by that Order has superior Authority for that family under the declared superiority Rule. Maximality is relative to that Order, Rule, family, and Scope; it is neither universal nor constituent.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D7 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D7 — Boundary Sovereignty:** Boundary Sovereignty is a scoped Configuration in which a Polity or other Entity Controls the Constraints governing a declared family of Transformations across its Boundary and an Order Enforces those Constraints. The profile requires at least one admitted and one blocked Transformation and a witnessed difference in Consequence or later State when Enforcement applies.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D8 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D8 — External Sovereignty:** External Sovereignty is an Order-indexed Relation in which an Order distinct from the target's internal Order recognizes an Entity or Polity as having Standing to participate in declared inter-Order Actions as its own Principal, rather than through an ActsFor Relation to another recognized Principal, under a Rule and Scope. It records external institutional status, not effective Control, internal Authority, or Constituent Power.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D9 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D9 — Preference:** Preference is a scoped asymmetric Relation in which an Agent, under a declared Rule, orders one candidate State or Consequence before another. It may be partial and need not be numerical, transitive outside its declared Scope, revealed by Action, or represented by a Utility Measure.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D10 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D10 — Utility Measure:** Utility Measure is a scoped Map that assigns candidates to a measure space under a Rule and Specification and orders those measures through a declared asymmetric Relation. It represents an Agent's Preference only when a separate correspondence witness proves that the induced ordering agrees with that Preference in the declared Scope.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-D11 type=proposed_definition -->
**QP-D11 — Price:** Price is a scoped exchange Relation recorded in a Ledger and admitted by an Order under a Rule, in which a Representation of consideration CountsAs the stated exchange condition for another Presence at a State. A Price is a Record of an offered, required, or recognized condition; it does not entail an exchange, Preference, Utility Measure, institutional valuation, or moral worth.

**QP-P1 — Institutional valuation:** Institutional valuation is an authorized projection of CountsAs in which an Order, under a Rule and Scope, assigns a valuation status to a Presence, Claim, Action, or Record. It is not a new term and does not entail Preference, Utility Measure, Price, Truth, or moral worth.

## Exact entailments and anti-entailments

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-C1 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**QP-C1 — Factivity and warrant remain cumulative:** Warranted Knowledge entails Factive Operative Knowledge, which entails Operative Knowledge and Truth for its exact carried Claim. The reverse implications fail separately. Operative Knowledge may carry a false Claim; a true Claim may lack an operative path; and Factive Operative Knowledge may lack Evidence admitted for its Claim and independently grounded for its interpreter. Evidence independent for another Agent does not close the warrant obligation.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-C2 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**QP-C2 — Moral discourse does not decide moral status:** Moral Status Attribution and Moral Personhood Designation do not entail their candidate condition. Non-attribution and non-designation do not decide it. Designation does not independently alter Personhood, Standing, protections, duties, Permissions, Interfaces, or Consequences; each downstream effect requires a separate Rule in a named Order and Scope.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-C3 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**QP-C3 — Sovereignty profiles do not compose silently:** Constituent, Constituted, Boundary, and External Sovereignty do not entail one another. No profile entails moral legitimacy, Truth, favorable Consequences, moral personhood, or generic Sovereignty. A Claim that several profiles form one Configuration must name every profile and connecting Rule.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-C4 type=anti_collapse_constraint -->
**QP-C4 — Valuation profiles remain distinct:** Preference, Utility Measure, Price, institutional valuation, and moral worth do not entail one another without an explicit correspondence Rule and witness. Action does not by itself reveal Preference; measurement does not create desire; recording a Price does not establish exchange or worth; institutional status does not create moral worth.

## Intellectual shadows

### Epistemology

The factive profile inherits the traditional requirement that knowledge cannot be false, while Gettier's challenge prevents justified true belief from being treated as automatically sufficient. Goldman's reliabilist program sharpens the need for a truth-conducive process. Organon does not settle the analysis of knowledge: it defines two local Configurations whose operative, truth, evidence, independence, and Admission joins are inspectable.

### Moral status and personhood

Kant's treatment of persons as ends and contemporary moral-status literature shadow the distinction between being morally considerable and being institutionally classified. Searle shadows the Designation layer only. Organon neither derives moral worth from rationality nor makes institutional status metaphysically constitutive; its contribution is preserving candidate, Claim, Designation, and downstream Rule as different layers.

### Sovereignty

Schmitt shadows exceptional decision, Hobbes the concentration of constituted authority, Negri constituent power, and international recognition practice the external profile. Organon does not reconcile their political theories. It prevents their local Relations from being smuggled into one property and makes each profile Order-, Rule-, and Scope-relative.

### Preference, utility, price, and valuation

Samuelson's revealed preference and von Neumann and Morgenstern's utility representation supply local rigor for ordering and measurement. Political economy and accounting distinguish price Records from broader theories of value; Searle shadows institutional valuation. Organon does not adopt rational-choice completeness, expected-utility axioms, a labor theory of value, or the claim that markets discover moral worth.

## Formal shadow

The noncanonical [QuarantineProfiles.lean](../ontology/formal/QuarantineProfiles.lean) supplies typed structures and finite witnesses for the promoted seams. It joins a true toy Claim to an existing Operative Knowledge instance; adds admitted Evidence for that same Claim while identifying the evidence claimant with the operative interpreter; separates moral candidate, Attribution, Designation, and downstream protection; constructs four profile-only sovereignty worlds in which each profile is inhabited and the other three are uninhabitable; and separates Preference, Utility Measure, Price, and institutional valuation in finite models.

The sovereignty structures encode the proposal's local seams rather than labels alone. Boundary Sovereignty requires distinct unenforced and enforced outcomes. External Sovereignty carries a Rule, Standing, an Action Scope containing the representative Action, participation as the target's own Principal, and explicit exclusion of `ActsFor` relations to every distinct Principal. The formal artifact proves only the exact anti-entailments represented by its countermodels: useful operation without factivity; Truth without an operative path; factivity without warrant; Attribution and Designation without candidate obtainment; non-designation with either candidate result; Designation without protection; every sovereignty profile without the other three; Preference without utility representation; Utility Measure without a modeled Preference; Price without exchange or moral worth; and institutional valuation without moral worth. It does not formalize universal moral truth, the totality of an Order, international law, core Action-to-choice attribution, expected utility, monetary arithmetic, or generic Knowledge, Sovereignty, or Value.

## Promotion gates that remain open

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-G1 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**QP-G1 — Core evidence parity:** The formal Evidence predicates remain proposal-local, but `claimantMatches` enforces that their claimant is the operative interpreter. Full parity requires constructing Warranted Knowledge from Organon's `IndependentFor`, Admissibility Rule provenance, Observation, Evidence, and Admission structures without a free substitute predicate.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-G2 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**QP-G2 — Moral condition and moral worth:** No binding Specification of the underlying moral condition or moral worth is supplied. Moral Status Attribution and Moral Personhood Designation remain discourse machinery only.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-G3 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**QP-G3 — Sovereignty realization and composition:** The finite profile predicates do not yet derive Authority, Control, Enforcement, or Recognition from complete Organon Orders. Generic Sovereignty remains blocked unless a corpus use requires a Configuration joining named profiles under explicit Rules.

<!-- organon:proposal-statement QP-G4 type=open_formalization_gate -->
**QP-G4 — Valuation semantics:** The formal model does not prove preference from behavior, representation by a Utility Measure, market exchange from Price, or moral worth from any valuation profile. Generic Value remains blocked because the profiles are independently satisfiable.

The underlying consciousness condition also remains absent. Bare Knowledge remains blocked on a justified genus spanning propositional knowledge, acquaintance, collective knowledge, practiced Capability, Factive Operative Knowledge, and Warranted Knowledge. Warranted Knowledge is Order-relative as defined here; whether a noninstitutional warrant profile is needed remains open.

## Promotion decision

Promote QP-D1 through QP-D11, QP-P1, and QP-C1 through QP-C4. Keep the six unresolved words and conditions named in the Verdict quarantined. The promoted profiles may be used only with their declared dependencies, Scope, and Rules; capitalization of the generic words remains nonbinding.
