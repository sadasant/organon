# Essay Corpus

This ledger is the public evidence surface for essays cited by the archived ontology extraction and the current ontology's provenance. It replaces links into Daniel's private Parergon directory structure with stable publication URLs and explicit recovery status.

The ledger does not copy the private canonical Markdown corpus into Organon. A public article is evidence that the published text exists; it does not prove that every local transcription is lossless. The recovery status records what the private corpus audit had established when this ledger was created.

## Audit boundary

The private Medium corpus audit counted 47 verified publication URLs and 33 complete canonical notes on 2026-08-01. Those figures were lower bounds, not a proof of completeness. Organon publishes only the subset required to inspect the ontology's stated evidence lineage.

When a cited article changes location, update its public URL here rather than introducing a dependency on a private filesystem path. When a previously incomplete body becomes losslessly recovered, update the recovery status and record the evidence used for that promotion.
