# Organon v0.13.0

Organon v0.13 separates three words that coordination systems routinely let impersonate one another.

## What changed

- Added Truth as materially adequate Claim-to-Reality correspondence under a declared Rule and truth-condition Specification, independent of Evidence, proof, access, consensus, or Admission.
- Added Trust as causal dependence admitted by a Constraint maintained in the trusting Entity's Boundary when another Entity's future contribution remains outside determining Control; involuntary vulnerability alone is not Trust.
- Added Alignment as profile-scoped conformity between ordered subject and target roles under a constructive Specification, without depending on temporal Direction.
- Added binding constraints C14-C16 for truth-status, trust-confidence-control, alignment-totalization, and cross-relation anti-collapses.
- Added the optional `correspondence-and-coordination` adoption profile.
- Added explicit corpus provenance and local intellectual shadows from semantic truth, philosophical trust, and interactive AI alignment.

## Verification target

- Canonical Markdown ontology: one readable file
- Semantic registry and provenance: 90 terms, 26 typed commitments
- Existing example adoption: 3 selected profiles
- Lean 4.30.0 formal shadow: per-Claim material adequacy; a true Claim without modeled Agent access; accepted and involuntary causal dependences over explicit participants; a false Claim whose own Representation aligns under a profile; and one joint Claim-Trust-Alignment situation

The Markdown ontology remains canonical. The finite models test the proposed seams without claiming a complete formal bridge to Reality, Action-derived contribution attribution, canonical Interior crossing or Consequence, or composition among Alignment profiles.
