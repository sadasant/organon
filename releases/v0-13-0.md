# Organon v0.13.0

Organon v0.13 separates three words that coordination systems routinely let impersonate one another.

## What changed

- Added Truth as Claim-to-Reality correspondence under a declared truth-condition Specification, independent of Evidence, proof, access, consensus, or Admission.
- Added Trust as accepted exposure to another Entity's future contribution when the trusting Entity cannot determine that contribution through its Control.
- Added Alignment as directional, profile-scoped subject-to-target conformity under a constructive Specification.
- Added binding constraints C14-C16 for truth-status, trust-confidence-control, alignment-totalization, and cross-relation anti-collapses.
- Added the optional `correspondence-and-coordination` adoption profile.
- Added explicit corpus provenance and local intellectual shadows from semantic truth, philosophical trust, and interactive AI alignment.

## Verification target

- Canonical Markdown ontology: one readable file
- Semantic registry and provenance: 90 terms, 26 typed commitments
- Existing example adoption: 3 selected profiles
- Lean 4.30.0 formal shadow: a true Claim without modeled Agent access; an actual trustee-introduced Transformation on a Causal path without confidence or Permission; profile-scoped Alignment without identity or Truth; and simultaneous inhabitation without collapse

The Markdown ontology remains canonical. The finite models test the proposed seams without claiming a complete formal bridge to Reality, a complete Interior-and-Boundary account of Trust Exposure, or composition among Alignment profiles.
