# Organon v0.20.0

Organon v0.20 promotes eight terms for embodied self-direction, target-guided activity, attention, and interpersonal relation while leaving Consciousness and the proposed Authority revision unchanged.

## What changed

- Added Embodied Self-Governance, Intention, Attention, Sustained Attention, Absolute Attention, Love, Care, and Respect as D112-D119.
- Added C39-C46 to preserve partial self-governance, intention without achievement, partial versus absolute Attention, the focus-relative Action limit, normative neutrality, and the separations among Love, Care, Respect, benefit, agreement, and affection.
- Defined Attention as an explicit finite organized-to-available channel ratio evaluated only at the current snapshot. Declared response comparisons hold context fixed, reproduce the current result under the focus, and change it under the contrast; they do not search historical Causal Contributions.
- Enforced unique channel counting, a positive denominator and bounded numerator, state-indexed attention rejecting both past-only and future results, order-independent absolute coverage, and exact-action Respect protection.
- Closed the numerator through exact causal-organization coverage, independently of the counted list; a genuinely focus-independent Action distinguishes the partial witness from Absolute Attention.
- Defined Love through sustained exact target Denotation, Care through an organized Action channel aimed at another Entity's indexed State, and Respect through a scoped Action Constraint with a typed protection witness.
- Added the optional `attention-and-relation` profile and synchronized registry, provenance, prompt, formal, adoption, algebra, and evaluation surfaces.

- Made Attention and Absolute Attention explicitly relative to a declared inventory and availability Specification; channel identity is independent of kind/result. Otherness uses local snapshot-stable participant classification, with shared-criterion witnesses for Love, Care, and Respect. Removed unproved Intention mode tags and preserved the entrainment decision.

## Promotion boundary

All eight terms are binding in their exact definitions in this proposed release. The Lean shadow uses declared local channel projections for Perception, Interpretation, Action, Agency, and Capability, and a snapshot-local counterfactual projection of causal organization; its schema-v2 dependency ledger records those representations and keeps canonical parity open. It does not define benefit, consent, morality, goodness, reciprocity, agreement, affection, or Consciousness. Promotion review remains blocked on Difference orientation and participant individuation; the snapshot repair does not close those findings.

## Verification target

- The registry contains 122 terms, including 119 definitions, and 57 typed commitments, including C1-C46.
- Every promoted definition has an exact Lean promotion contract, and four anti-entailments have finite proofs.
- The proposal manifest accounts for every dependency as formalized, represented, prose-only, or outside the formal boundary.
- The Lean project builds without `sorry`, `admit`, or undeclared axioms.
- Generated prompt, formal-audit, reduction, and positive-calculus surfaces account for all 122 registered terms.
