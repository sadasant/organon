# Organon v0.20.0

Organon v0.20 promotes Attention, Care, and Respect while leaving Consciousness
and the proposed Authority revision unchanged.

## What changed

- Attention (D114) is a persistent directed Relation of Perception or Interpretation
  organized by Differences in another Entity across its State changes, within Scope.
  It permits narrow focus, cessation, and either unilateral or reciprocal relations.
  A finite channel ratio and fixed Representation/contrast pair are not required.
- Sustained Attention (D115) is withdrawn because persistence belongs to Attention.
  Love (D117) is withdrawn, not renamed or identified with Attention. Ordinary love
  remains undefined; particular uses may involve Attention, Care, Meaning, or Ritual
  without promoting a universal combination. Neither identifier is reassigned.
- Care (D118) is a Relation in which Attention to another Entity's changing State
  conditions selection or adjustment of Action under a Specification of Consequences
  concerning that same Entity. The criterion must be operative; neither successful
  conformity nor benefit is required.
- Respect (D119) constrains Action through the other's expressed conditions or
  contextual Interpretation. Expressed conditions override conflicting defaults
  within Scope; no explicit negotiation or Ritual is required. Accepted temporary
  Agency limitation remains bounded, and accidental violation is not deliberate
  disregard. Identity/option protection alone no longer defines Respect. Neither
  Care nor Respect makes formal claims about undefined Love. C41, C43, C45, and C46
  retain their applicable limits.
- C44 is retired with Love. C42's full-coverage theorem survives solely in the
  nonbinding finite measurement. Historical Attention, SustainedAttention, Love,
  and Care records and their existing proofs remain under `NonbindingExamples`.
- The earlier withdrawals of Intention (D113/C40), Fully Exercisable Permission
  (D061), Constituted Exercise (D077), Embodied Self-Governance (D112/C39), and
  Absolute Attention (D116) remain in effect, with no identifier reassignment.
  Their constructions survive only as examples. Standing distinguishes placement
  from Recognition's recording; Constituent Exercise and sovereignty are unchanged.
- The nonbinding [Self/Auto proposal](../proposals/self-auto.md) remains. D111 now
  distinguishes rejection as a contrasting possibility from prior occurrence.
  Its retained Lean endpoint construction is a restricted temporal witness, not
  complete canonical selection. No generic Auto/Autonomy is promoted.
- Registry, profile, provenance, prompt, formal audit, algebra, and evaluation
  source snapshots follow the revised vocabulary. Historical reviews are preserved.

## Formal boundary and verification target

The registry contains 115 terms (112 definitions) and 53 commitments, including
C1-C38, C41, C43, C45, and C46. The proposal has three definitions and four constraints.

Eight `AttentionRevision.lean` theorems test a supplied response model, including
covariation rejection and a narrow-focus counterexample. They do not construct
canonical Perception/Interpretation/Causal Contribution or prove continuity between
samples. Attention, Care, and their constraints are explicitly outside the complete
formal boundary. Attention's contract projects the new tracking predicate; Care's
partial contract projects response and Specification sensitivity. Five bounded
`CareRevision.lean` theorems separate criterion-governed responsiveness from success,
reject an ignored criterion, and reject success alone. Canonical Attention, Action,
Consequence, causal comparison paths, and the same-Entity target join remain unproved.
Seven `RespectRevision.lean` theorems test condition priority, defaults, participant
and Scope matching, bounded permission, and actual versus planned intrusion.
Respect's boundary-qualified contract projects scoped condition resolution, not
condition provenance, consent, contextual accuracy, or causal regulation. The old
identity/option record and its proofs remain historical nonbinding examples.
Compilation does not establish empirical application.

The [attention review](../reviews/promotion/attention-love-care.json) and
[embodiment review](../reviews/promotion/embodied-consciousness.json) attest current
implementation sources and contracts with the boundaries above. Required source
pins now include every declared auxiliary formal evidence file. D111's extra
temporal restriction is exposed by a general theorem, not promoted as a necessary
condition of rejection. Model judgments have not been rerun; downstream evaluations
remain incomplete/hold. Local promotion readiness does not establish complete
Lean/prose parity or downstream model quality.
