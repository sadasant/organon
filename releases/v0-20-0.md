# Organon v0.20.0

Organon v0.20 promotes five terms for attention and interpersonal relation while leaving Consciousness and the proposed Authority revision unchanged.

## What changed

- Added the nonbinding [Self/Auto proposal](../proposals/self-auto.md) and preserved historical candidate sources for Daniel's review. The former D112 construction retains Configuration typing as an example; D111 joins exact alternative occurrences and constructive availability. The inhabited internal-governance profile is not a generic Auto/Autonomy promotion.

- Added Attention, Sustained Attention, Love, Care, and Respect as D114-D115 and D117-D119.
- Withdrew Constituted Exercise (D077), Embodied Self-Governance (D112), Absolute Attention (D116), and C39 without identifier reassignment. Authority-backed Action remains an example, the exact bodily-selection record is nonbinding, and full coverage is a derived Attention condition. Standing and Recognition retain their names, with placement separated from recording in Lean and the algebra. Constituent Exercise and the derived Constituent Sovereignty profile remain unchanged.
- Withdrew Intention (D113) and C40 after the termhood review. Their construction remains a [nonbinding example](../ontology/formal/RepresentationActivityExample.lean), with general inverse conversions and freely variable referents; neither identifier is reassigned and no replacement term is promoted.
- Withdrew Fully Exercisable Permission (D061). The [nonbinding permission-coverage example](../ontology/formal/Model.lean) fixes one Permission, time, and resource Configuration and separates individual feasibility from funding both Actions. D061 is not reassigned; Permission and Exercisability remain binding.
- Added C41-C46 to preserve partial versus full-coverage Attention, the focus-relative Action limit, normative neutrality, and the separations among Love, Care, Respect, benefit, agreement, and affection.
- Defined Attention as an explicit finite organized-to-available channel ratio evaluated only at the current snapshot. Declared response comparisons hold context fixed, reproduce the current result under the focus, and change it under the contrast; they do not search historical Causal Contributions.
- Enforced unique channel counting, a positive denominator and bounded numerator, state-indexed attention rejecting both past-only and future results, order-independent absolute coverage, and exact-action Respect protection.
- Closed the numerator through exact causal-organization coverage, independently of the counted list. The partial Action follows a separate input and remains unchanged under the attended contrast; reversed labels or failed organization alone do not establish independence.
- Defined Love through sustained exact target Denotation, Care through an organized Action channel aimed at another Entity's indexed State, and Respect through a scoped Action Constraint with a typed protection witness.
- Added the optional `attention-and-relation` profile and synchronized registry, provenance, prompt, formal, adoption, algebra, and evaluation surfaces.

- Made Attention and its full-coverage condition explicitly relative to a declared inventory and availability Specification; channel identity is independent of kind/result. Otherness uses record-independent classification over identity Invariant and Boundary. Shared-criterion witnesses for Love, Care, and Respect differ in Boundary behavior, not history length; the old prefix-record witness is rejected. Removed unproved Intention mode tags and preserved the entrainment decision.

## Promotion boundary

All five terms are binding in their exact definitions in this proposed release. The Lean shadow uses declared local channel projections for Perception, Interpretation, Action, Agency, and Capability, and a snapshot-local counterfactual projection of causal organization; its schema-v2 dependency ledger records those representations and keeps canonical parity open. Independence is relative to the named contrast and declared response domain, not a proof of physical completeness. Record-independent participant classification closes the prefix-record counterexample, not canonical numerical identity: equal classes do not prove one Entity, Boundary differences need not force different classes, and continuity across Boundary changes remains gated. It does not define benefit, consent, morality, goodness, reciprocity, agreement, affection, ordinary intention, or Consciousness.
