# Organon v0.17.0

Organon v0.17 adds the recurrent enactment mechanism through which participant-indexed Meaning can obtain and persist without adding a primitive.

## What changed

- Strengthened Flow with distinct ordered occurrences, one object-level recurrence Relation, Scope, and named Persistence witness instead of introducing duplicate Recurrence. Constructive Rule and Specification belong to Flow Claims and participant-relative Ritual Interpretation rather than Flow's ontic obtainment.
- Added Ritual as participant-bound recurrent practice in which retained Memory makes a contrastive Difference to later Interpretation and the sustaining Causal Contribution passes through that Interpretation; one participant is sufficient.
- Added Meaning as the Relation historically constituted by Ritual and currently maintained by qualifying enactment or an actually joined ritual-derived contribution, not significance stored inside its target or Records.
- Made the normative neutrality explicit: addiction and trauma loops can qualify when all Ritual joins obtain, while repetition, compulsion, distress, or diagnosis alone remains insufficient; Ritual and Meaning imply neither benefit nor endorsement.
- Reduced ritual-derived effects to existing Record, Memory, Change, Consequence, Environment, Configuration, and Causal Contribution machinery rather than promoting Ritual Residue.
- Added C25-C31 and the optional `ritual-meaning` adoption profile.
- Kept Beauty, Play, Love, goodness, consent, consciousness, and moral worth distinct and unresolved.

## Verification target

- Canonical Markdown ontology: one readable file
- Semantic registry and provenance: 109 terms, 42 typed commitments
- Lean 4.30.0 formal shadow: repeated Flow containing its own Scope, occurrence-membership proofs, object-level recurrence predicate, and no classifier; a separate nondegenerate `FlowClassification` whose Scope matches the Flow Scope, which joins constructive Specification to recurrence and rejects one in-Scope non-occurrence; position-indexed participant-history access for every occurrence and strictly earlier uptake for every noninitial occurrence; a constructive `RitualUptake` in which both indexed occurrences conform and recur while contrastive Perception and admissible Memory each change Interpretation; unequal target States inside one target Persistence history and Invariant plus one rejected substitution State; private Ritual; Meaning support joined to an actual uptake by equality or downstream origin; stable participant indexing with equal-current-State nonidentity; Flow and single-access structural insufficiency examples; derived-effect maintenance and dormancy classifiers; and causally propagated non-identical Meaning Relations

The Markdown ontology remains canonical. The Lean shadow uses proposal-local State roles for Perception, Memory, and Interpretation because OrganonCore does not yet expose their canonical structures. It also does not formalize represented-target Denotation, elapsed decay, Beauty, Play, Love, goodness, consent, consciousness, or moral worth.
