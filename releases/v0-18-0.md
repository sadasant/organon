# Organon v0.18.0

Organon v0.18 makes definition admission explicit after a complete
falsification-first reduction audit.

## What changed

- Strengthened C1 by separating lexical dependency closure from instance-level
  definition admission. `depends_on` records vocabulary used anywhere in the
  complete logical form; it does not make every referenced term a positive
  condition of every instance.
- Required a type- and index-consistent interpretation satisfying the complete
  logical form, including applicable premises, quantifiers, alternatives,
  exclusions, and required witnesses.
- Explicitly rejected classification from dependency presence, label
  resemblance, or assertion of the target alone.
- Retained all 109 stable terms and all 42 typed commitments. No term meaning,
  identifier, or dependency list changed.

## Experimental basis

The nonbinding candidate algebra identifies six human-authored candidate
disciplines across the tested anti-collapse surface. Each has one isolating
labeled fixture, but the checker uses them as annotations rather than executing
them as inference laws. Its complete audit accounts for every term and
commitment and shows that the taxonomy organizes admissibility failures but
cannot generate positive vocabulary.

A follow-on registry-level control wraps all 106 definitions and exercises 825
dependency-removal fixtures. It is deliberately degenerate: it conflates
lexical references with positive premises and its conformity witness is opaque
to the finite evaluator. Its 825 dependency-removal cases are structural
query-regression checks of wrapper sensitivity, not proofs of ontological
necessity. The control and six candidate disciplines therefore remain
experimental. Only the separation of lexical dependency from complete
definition admission enters C1.
