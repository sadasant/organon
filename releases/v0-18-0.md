# Organon v0.18.0

Organon v0.18 makes definition admission explicit after a complete
falsification-first reduction audit.

## What changed

- Strengthened C1: declared dependencies are necessary but not sufficient for
  a registered classification.
- Required one typed, index-consistent assignment to satisfy the complete
  binding definition, including every named premise and witness.
- Explicitly rejected classification from dependency presence, label
  resemblance, or assertion of the target alone.
- Retained all 109 stable terms and all 42 typed commitments. No term meaning,
  identifier, or dependency list changed.

## Experimental basis

The nonbinding candidate algebra identifies six ablation-essential laws across
the tested anti-collapse surface. Its complete audit accounts for every term
and commitment and shows that the six laws constrain admissibility but cannot
generate positive vocabulary.

A follow-on registry-level search finds a one-constructor cardinality minimum:
generic witnessed-definition introduction admits all 106 definitions and
survives 825 one-step dependency and witness removals. It fails semantic
anti-vacuity because the conformity witness is opaque to the finite evaluator.
The constructor and six laws therefore remain experimental. Only their shared
definition-admission invariant enters C1.
