# Organon v0.11.0

Organon v0.11 distinguishes a Representation acquiring causal force from a Representation becoming true, identical to its target, or equal to Reality.

## What changed

- Added Operationalization: a Representation incorporated into Rules and Interfaces so that it can condition Transformations in a scoped Causal path.
- Added binding constraint C11, preventing causal efficacy from collapsing into representational identity, Map fidelity, or Evidence.
- Added the optional `operational-representation` adoption profile.
- Added explicit provenance from `The Conspiracy of Reason`, `Reasoning and Language`, and `Computer Science is a Social Science`.
- Recorded `The Higher You Go` in the corpus surface while leaving Trust quarantined because its predictive, relational, and delegative senses remain unresolved.

## Verification target

- Canonical Markdown ontology: one readable file
- Semantic registry and provenance: 85 terms, 21 typed commitments
- Existing example adoption: 3 profiles, unchanged dependency closure
- Lean v0.10 formal sources and receipt: unchanged and still verified
- Explicit formalization gap: Operationalization and C11

The Markdown ontology remains canonical. The new term is proposed from corpus evidence, not presented as a theorem extracted from the essays.
