# Organon v0.15.0

Organon v0.15 governs the remaining quarantined vocabulary without pretending that its generic conditions have been solved.

## What changed

- Added Factive Operative Knowledge and Warranted Knowledge as cumulative profiles over Operative Knowledge, Truth, independently grounded Evidence, and Order-relative Admission.
- Added Moral Status Attribution and Moral Personhood Designation while keeping the candidate moral condition and moral worth undefined.
- Added Constituent, Constituted, Boundary, and External Sovereignty as independently satisfiable profiles rather than one sovereign property.
- Added Preference, Utility Measure, and Price; institutional valuation remains an authorized CountsAs projection, not a duplicate term.
- Added binding constraints C20-C23 and four optional adoption profiles.
- Retained the underlying consciousness condition, bare Knowledge, underlying moral personhood, generic Sovereignty, generic Value, and moral worth in quarantine.

## Verification target

- Canonical Markdown ontology: one readable file
- Semantic registry and provenance: 104 terms, 34 typed commitments
- Existing example adoption: 3 selected profiles
- Lean 4.30.0 formal shadow: accurate and mistaken operative Claims; open and closed Evidence admission; obtaining and non-obtaining moral candidates; four separately witnessed sovereignty profiles; and distinct Preference, Utility Measure, Price, and institutional-valuation models

The Markdown ontology remains canonical. The finite models test exact seams and anti-entailments. They do not establish universal moral truth, complete institutional parity for proposal-local predicates, international legal sufficiency, preference revelation, market clearing, moral worth, or generic definitions of Knowledge, Sovereignty, and Value.
