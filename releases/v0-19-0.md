# Organon v0.19.0

Organon v0.19 promotes the dependency-closed embodiment foundation while
keeping the proposed consciousness condition nonbinding.

## What changed

- Added Body, Bodily Organization, Embodied Perspective, Recurrent
  Integration, and Internal Activity Selection as D107-D111.
- Added C32-C38 to preserve the distinctions tested by the proposal's seven
  countermodels: identity may survive part replacement; Boundary need not
  isolate; shared enclosure or signals do not establish organization or
  integration; perspective does not establish selection or the candidate;
  candidate obtainment does not establish speech, outward Action, complete
  Control, Attribution, Designation, Standing, or moral status.
- Added the optional `embodied-integration` profile and synchronized registry,
  provenance, adoption, prompt, formal-audit, and algebra surfaces.
- Recorded bounded constituent inclusion and process-family identity in the
  definitions without claiming a universal mereology or process ontology.

## Promotion boundary

The five supporting terms are binding. Their conjunction supplies one
proposal-local candidate condition for consciousness, but v0.19 does not define
universal Consciousness, establish that any Entity satisfies that candidate,
or alter the existing separation among candidate obtainment, Consciousness
Attribution, and Consciousness Designation.

The Lean shadow remains noncanonical. It provides an inhabited finite witness
and seven countermodels for the promoted anti-entailments; compilation proves
the encoding elaborates under the pinned toolchain, not exact parity with the
binding prose or application to an external Entity.
